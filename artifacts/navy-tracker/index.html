P15B is done. The destroyer gap the Navy is trying to fill is P15C, not another Visakhapatnam. P18 / 18A is the large surface combatant after that, and on current talk does not arrive before the mid-2040s — which is why P15C exists at all.

The four clocks that matter

Your model: RFP, then about three years to first steel, then 6–8 years for the lead ship, using P17A for frigates and destroyers and Kalvari for the submarine. P17B has already broken the old “RFP in H1 2027” assumption — the RFP went out on 16 September 2026. Everything after contract is still a projection.

Follow-on to Nilgiri. Public descriptions: more VLS than P17A, better signature, strike + area air defence + ASW. Weapons and sensors still have foreign content risk even if the hull is competed at home. Earlier July 2026 NDTV line (RFP in 18 months, six ships) is overtaken.

How the dates are made

Rules used on this page

  • P17B and P15C: construction starts about three years after RFP. Lead ship 6–8 years after that. P17A is the reference — Feb 2015 contract, Nilgiri delivered Dec 2024, follow-on build time falling from 93 months toward 80.
  • P75I: contractual first boat seven years after signature, then about one a year. Kalvari is the cautionary reference, not the schedule: twelve years to the first boat, nineteen to the sixth.
  • NGC: corvette, so a shorter band — 5–7 years from contract to lead ship. Contract itself is the open item.
  • A signed contract moves the bar from “model” to “contractual.” Nothing on the future tab has that signature except NGMV, which is already in steel and lives on the yards tab.
  • P17B RFP of 16 Sep 2026 replaces the older “H1 2027” expectation. If you still want the page to show the pre-RFP model as a ghost bar, that is a one-line change in the data.

What “live” can actually mean

  • The Navy, MDL, GRSE, GSL and CSL do not publish a machine-readable build feed. PIB notes, yard disclosures and the occasional CNC leak are the sources.
  • This file is a snapshot with the data in one script block at the bottom. Updating a date is a text edit, not a scrape.
  • A hosted version on GitHub Pages or any static host is the practical “live site.” A bot that files a commit when PIB posts a commissioning note is the closest thing to automatic, and it will still need a human on the projection bars.
